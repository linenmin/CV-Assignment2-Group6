EoMT-DINOv3 data flow on a 512 × 512 input
Figure 1. EoMT-DINOv3 (Kerssies et al., 2025) attached to a frozen DINOv3 ViT-L/16 backbone. The blue blocks copy pretrained DINOv3 weights and stay frozen during PASCAL VOC fine-tuning; the orange blocks (last two ViT layers plus 100 learnable mask queries) are the only trainable parameters. Green blocks produce per-query mask and class logits, fused into a single semantic map by an argmax over queries followed by bilinear upsampling.
